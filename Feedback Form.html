AI agentThanks. Before we go deeper, how do you decide which of those challenges to take on first when everything feels urgent?
EmilyI look at blast radius first. Anything touching money movement gets priority, then whatever blocks the most people. I write that ranking down so the team can push back on it.
AI agentLet's move to the retry pipeline. What made duplicate charges hard to eliminate in the first place?
EmilySorry, could you repeat that?Right, the retries. The hard part was that a timeout didn't tell us whether the charge had landed, so we couldn't retry blindly. We introduced idempotency keys so a retried request would be recognised and dropped.
AI agentAnd how did you explain that change to the partner teams who depended on the old behaviour?Let me rephrase: what did you do to bring the partner teams along?
EmilyI put together a doc, and there were some meetings. It was mostly the same information repeated in different formats until it stuck.
AI agentThat sounds impactful. Could you walk me through how you decided where to put the cache, and what you would monitor once it was live?
EmilyWe put it in front of the ledger reads, since those were the hot path and tolerant of a short staleness window. Writes stayed synchronous.For monitoring I watched hit rate, tail latency, and stale-read counts. If hit rate dropped below 80% we knew the key design was wrong.
