AI Interview Center Slack notifications

Slack carries the moments that need a person today; email carries digests and records. The same event never goes to both. Four messages in total — two for recruiters, two for hiring managers — each batched, capped and deep-linked into the leaderboard.

Cases and cadence

Every message is a direct message from the AI Interviewer app. Nothing sends between 7:00 pm and 8:00 am local or on weekends — it waits for the next window. Recruiters can mute any position; hiring managers can hand a request back.
